Everything you need to know

Here you’ll find the important details about our services, rates and how to book with us.

Q1: What services do you offer?

We offer bridal packages, make up, threading, bleach, facials, hair treatment, hair spa, hair cutting, hair colour, pedicure, manicure, waxing and mehandi. We also provide nail extension, hair extension, eyelash extension, micro blading, pigmentation, saree wearing and ear gun stud piercing, along with henna and face whitening cream sale.

Q2: How much do the bridal packages cost?

As per our 2025 rate list: Normal Bridal Package ₹20,000, Classic with Jewellery ₹22,000, HD with Jewellery ₹25,000 and Air brush with Jewellery ₹40,000.

Q3: Do you do mehandi and Arabic designs?

Yes. We do Arabic mehandi designs in palm wrist, bangle, elbow and elbow-to-upper-arm lengths, for one side or both sides. Rates start from ₹250.

Q4: Do you provide nail, hair and eyelash extensions?

Yes. Nail extension is ₹1,500, hair extension is ₹5,000 and eyelash extension ranges from ₹1,000 to ₹3,000. We also offer micro blading (₹10,000) and pigmentation (₹3,000).

Q5: Where are you located and how can I book?

We are near Alappuzha East – Vandanam Medical College. Call us on 7356 493 720 or 9946 213 456 to book your appointment.
